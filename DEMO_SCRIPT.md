## Recording Tips

1. **Pre-open all tabs** before recording: Career Command Center view, Anthropic entry, TechStartup entry, Agent chat
2. **Use keyboard shortcuts** to navigate — looks polished, saves time
3. **Record at 1080p minimum** — judges watch on big screens
4. **No pauses** — every second counts at 60s
5. **Speak fast but clear** — NYC energy, confident, builder's voice
6. **Don't explain what Notion is** — 5 of 12 judges work there
